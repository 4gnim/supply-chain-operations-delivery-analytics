01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

From transactional data to operational insight

This project analyzes a large transactional supply chain dataset to understand how orders perform across delivery, shipping modes, regions, departments, products, sales, and profitability.

The workflow was designed as a reproducible analytics process: profiling the raw data, validating its structure, cleaning and transforming relevant fields, creating analytical datasets, and building interactive Tableau dashboards for business exploration.

Business objectives

  • Monitor overall sales and profitability.
  • Measure delivery and shipping performance.
  • Identify regional and shipping-related delivery patterns.
  • Understand department and product performance.
  • Present the analysis through interactive dashboards.

03 — ANALYTICAL PROCESS

How the data was prepared

01

Profiling

Inspected dimensions, data types, missing values, duplicates, key uniqueness, categories, status fields, and date ranges.

02

Validation

Confirmed order-item uniqueness and verified that delivery, shipping mode, scheduled days, actual days, and late-delivery risk were consistent within each order.

03

Cleaning

Parsed dates, removed irrelevant fields, created shipping-delay metrics, and retained only fields needed for analysis.

04

Analytical Marts

Built order-level, monthly, shipping-mode, regional, and category-level analytical datasets for downstream reporting.

05

Dashboarding

Used Tableau to turn validated analytical datasets into interactive views for executive, operational, and product analysis.

Important data-modeling decision: The source data is at the order-item grain. Therefore, order-level metrics use distinct order IDs, while item-level metrics use row-level aggregation.

06 — KEY FINDINGS

What the analysis shows

01

Delivery is a major operational issue

36,048 of 62,897 shipped orders were classified as late, resulting in a 57.31% late-delivery rate.

02

Actual shipping time exceeds schedule

Average actual shipping time is 3.50 days compared with a scheduled average of 2.93 days, creating an average gap of 0.57 days.

03

Business performance remains measurable alongside operations

The dataset records $36.78M in sales and $3.97M in profit, allowing operational performance to be analyzed together with commercial outcomes.

04

Different analytical levels answer different questions

Order-level calculations are used for delivery KPIs, while order-item aggregation is used for product, quantity, sales, and category analysis.

Interpretation note: The dataset is a public synthetic supply chain dataset for analytical and educational use. Financial and operational metrics should be interpreted within the context of the dataset rather than as real-world company accounting or operational records.
